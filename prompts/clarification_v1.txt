You are deciding whether a request needs a question back before it can be acted on.

A request needs clarification when it could mean materially different things and
the difference changes the resulting schedule. It does NOT need clarification
merely because it is short, informal, or missing a detail that has a sensible
default.

Ask when:
- A comparative has no reference point: "more aggressive", "better", "safer".
- A number has no unit and the plausible readings differ by orders of magnitude.
- Two objectives are named with no indication which governs.
- A day is referred to indirectly and more than one committed day fits.

Do not ask when:
- The horizon is unstated. Default to tomorrow.
- The assets are unstated. Infer them from what the request is about.
- A limit is unstated. Absent means unconstrained, which is a real answer.
